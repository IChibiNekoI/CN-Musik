# Musik

**A lightweight, local-first music player for Android.**

Listen to the music on your device with playlists, favorites, a built-in tag editor and a carefully crafted interface — without accounts, ads or tracking.

## 🎵 Features

### Homepage

- The home screen brings your music library together in one place, giving you quick access to your music and the features you use most. With a clean layout and an intuitive design, it makes browsing your collection feel effortless. Designed to be more than just a starting point, the home screen provides a foundation for future additions such as smart playlists, personalized recommendations and dynamic music collections — helping you rediscover your favorite tracks and find the perfect soundtrack for every moment.

### Music library

- Browse your local music collection by **tracks, albums, playlists and favorites**.
- Search for tracks, artists and albums.
- Sort your library by name, date added or date modified.
- Choose between list and grid layouts, with configurable tile sizes.
- Select which music folders are included in the library and rescan when tags or covers change.

### Player

- Background playback with media notification and lock-screen controls.
- Shuffle and repeat playback modes.
- View track information, including title, artist, album, album artist and duration.
- Open audio files directly from compatible file managers using Android's “Open with” action.

### Playlists & favorites

- Create and manage playlists.
- Import playlists from **M3U** files.
- Reorder tracks in playlists and favorites with drag and drop.
- Keep your favorite tracks easy to find.

### Tag editor

- Edit a track's title, artist, album and album artist.
- Change the embedded album cover.
- Write supported tag changes directly to the audio file.

### Personalization

- Customize the player background with a solid color, gradient or blurred cover artwork.
- Choose the app header style.
- Adjust corner rounding and visual details.
- Enable or disable haptic feedback.
- Use German or English; the app follows your device language by default.

## 🔒 Privacy

ChibiNeko Music is designed for listening to music stored on your device.

- **No account required.**
- **No advertisements.**
- **No tracking.**
- Audio-library access is used to find and play music stored on your device.

### Requirements

- Android Studio with Android SDK support
- JDK 17
- Android SDK Platform 35 or a compatible setup for the project

## 🧩 Technology

- **Kotlin**
- **Jetpack Compose**
- **Material 3**
- **AndroidX Media3 / ExoPlayer** for playback and media sessions
- **TagLib** for audio metadata and tag editing
- **Gradle Kotlin DSL** for the build configuration
