ChibiNeko is a music player for the music stored on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Features:
- Library with views for tracks, albums, playlists and favorites
- Search and sorting by date added, date modified or name
- Create and import playlists
- Reorder tracks in playlists and favorites by long-pressing and dragging
- Track info: title, artist, album, album artist, duration and dates
- Edit tags (title, artist, album, album artist and album cover) directly in the file
- Background playback with a notification and lock screen controls
- Shuffle and repeat
- Customizable appearance

Privacy:
The app only reads audio files on your device. The F-Droid version does not check for updates itself, F-Droid takes care of that.
